Nossas Compras is a shopping list app shared among groups (family, friends, household), with a record of purchases and the splitting of costs among members. This policy explains which personal data the app processes, for what purpose, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Wagner dos Anjos, an individual, responsible for the Nossas Compras app.

Contact for any privacy matter, including exercising your rights: edob2anjos@gmail.com.

As a small-scale processing agent, the controller does not appoint a data protection officer (ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022); the communication channel with you and with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) is the e-mail above.

2. What data the app processes and why

Sign-up and account

  • Full name, what you want to be called, e-mail, password, date of birth; sex and profile photo, if you choose to provide them.
  • Why: to create and keep your account, to identify you to the members of your groups and to make the content suitable for your age (section 6).
  • The password is stored in encrypted form by the authentication service; no one, not even the person responsible for the app, can read it.
  • The language and theme you choose, so the app looks the way you want on any device.

Groups, lists and purchases

  • The groups you belong to and your role in them (administrator or member).
  • Lists and items: name, quantity, unit, price, note, item photo (optional), barcode and who added it.
  • Purchases: who is shopping, the place (store name or address, suggested by the app or typed by you), the items put in the cart and their prices.
  • Group accounts: how much each person spent, how much they owe and to whom, and the recorded payments.
  • Notices inside the app (e.g. "someone started a purchase") and your notice preferences for each group.
  • Why: this is the service itself — letting the group build lists, shop together and split the costs.

Invitations

  • When you invite someone to a group, you enter that person's e-mail and, optionally, a nickname. Only the group administrator sees that e-mail, and only while the invitation is pending.
  • Why: so that the invited person finds the invitation when they sign in to the app.

Location

  • When you start a purchase, if you allow it, the app uses the device's location to suggest the store name or address. You can deny the permission and type the place yourself.
  • When the location is obtained, the app also stores the purchase coordinates. They are not shown to anyone — not to the group members, not to you. Purpose: a future feature of the app that will suggest places with lower prices near you, based on the purchase prices. This feature will not show prices or who bought, it will only suggest the place.

Camera and photos

  • The camera reads product barcodes. The image is processed on the device itself and is neither stored nor sent.
  • Profile and item photos are only sent when you choose them. The profile photo is reduced on the device before it is sent.

Barcodes and product database

  • When you scan a code, the app asks Open Food Facts (a public product database, in France) for the product name. In this query, Open Food Facts receives the scanned code and your device's IP address.
  • The app keeps its own "code → product name" database, shared among all users, which does not store who scanned the code or from which group.
  • When the product is not recognized and you type a name, that name is reviewed by the person responsible for the app before it enters the shared database, with no record of who suggested it. Do not use people's names or personal information in that field.

Data stored on your device

  • To work without internet, the app stores on the device a copy of your lists and purchases and the actions taken offline, until it can send them. When you sign out, the copy of the lists and purchases and the scanned codes are deleted from the device. Offline actions that have not been sent yet stay stored only on the device, so they are not lost, and are sent when you sign in again to the same account; another account signing in on the same device has no access to them.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (art. 7, V): the data needed for the app to work — account, groups, lists, purchases, accounts and notices.
  • Performance of a contract (art. 7, V) also for the barcode query to Open Food Facts: it is a feature you trigger when you scan a code.
  • Consent (art. 7, I): location, camera and photos, requested by the device's system, which you can deny or revoke in the settings.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX): non-personalized ads, which keep the app free, and the record of product queries used to limit abuse (deleted within 10 minutes, section 7).

4. Who the data is shared with

  • Members of your groups: see your nickname ("what you want to be called") and your photo, and what you do in the group (items you added, purchases, prices and payments). They do not see your full name, e-mail, date of birth or sex.
  • Supabase (server and database hosting): the data is kept on servers in São Paulo (Brazil).
  • Open Food Facts (France): barcode and IP address, in the product query (section 2).
  • Google AdMob (ads): the app shows non-personalized ads — it does not use your profile, your purchase data or history to choose ads. Even so, to display the ad, Google receives technical information from the device, such as the IP address. For minors, the app asks Google for age-appropriate treatment.
  • The system's location service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS): when suggesting the store, the coordinates are sent to the device system's address service.
  • The app does not sell personal data and does not share it with other companies for advertising.

5. International transfer

Some data is processed outside Brazil (art. 33 of the LGPD):

  • Open Food Facts (France): the European Union is recognized by the ANPD as a destination with adequate protection (Resolution CD/ANPD No. 32/2026).
  • Google (ads): the transfer follows the standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD, included in Google's data protection terms.
  • The system's address service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS): needed for the feature you requested, suggesting the store name (art. 33, IX).

6. Children and teenagers

  • The app can be used by people of any age. There is no account linked to a guardian and no parental control inside the app.
  • The date of birth is declared by you at sign-up and is used only to make the content suitable for your age: for people under 18, ads follow Google's rules for children and teenagers and do not show content that is inappropriate for the age. Age is never used to choose ads or offers for minors.
  • Parents and guardians who want to follow a minor's use can use the phone's own parental controls (such as Google Family Link or Apple Screen Time) and contact the person responsible for the app at the e-mail in section 8.

7. How long the data is kept

  • Account and group data: while the account exists.
  • Purchase coordinates: while the account exists.
  • When you delete the account (Me → Delete account): full name, e-mail, photo, sex and date of birth are deleted; you leave all groups; the coordinates of your purchases are deleted. In the group records (purchases, prices, debts) you keep appearing only by your nickname, so that the groups do not lose the history of their accounts.
  • Record of product queries (usage limit, against abuse): deleted within 10 minutes.
  • Notices inside the app: unread ones are deleted after 90 days; read ones, 30 days after being opened.
  • Invitations: the e-mail and nickname of the invited person are kept only while the invitation is pending (up to 30 days). When the invitation is accepted, canceled or expires, they are deleted.
  • Server backups: the server provider (Supabase) makes automatic backups of the database, kept for up to 7 days. Deleted data may remain in these backups for up to 7 days, unused, until they are replaced.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time (art. 18 of the LGPD):

  • confirm whether we process your data and access it;
  • correct incomplete or wrong data — most of it directly in the app, in Me → My profile;
  • request the anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in breach of the law;
  • request data portability — through the e-mail below, and you receive a copy of your data;
  • delete your account — directly in the app, in Me → Delete account;
  • know who your data is shared with;
  • revoke consents (location, camera and photos, in the device settings);
  • request the removal of a product name that contains personal information.

For anything that cannot be done in the app, write to edob2anjos@gmail.com. Confirmation that we process your data is answered in simplified form as soon as possible, and the complete answer (including the copy of the data) within 15 days of the request (art. 19).

9. Security

  • Communication between the app and the server is encrypted.
  • Access to data is controlled on the server: each person only reaches the data of the groups they belong to.
  • Photos are kept in private storage, accessed through temporary links.
  • The app does not store the camera image, and the purchase location is not shown to anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If the change is important, you also receive a notice in the app (in the Notices bell), with a summary of what changed.
