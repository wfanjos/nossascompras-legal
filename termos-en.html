2. What the app does

  • Shopping lists shared among the members of a group (family, friends, household).
  • A record of the purchases made from those lists, with the items and prices typed by whoever bought them.
  • Splitting of costs among those who bought, when the group chooses to use shared accounts, and a record of who paid whom.
  • Barcode scanning to recognize products, with a product database shared among all users.

The app does not move money. Splitting costs is only a record to help the group get organized: the app does not charge, pay, guarantee or intermediate any payment between members. A debt only shows as paid when the person who received the money confirms it in the app.

4. Groups

  • Whoever creates a group is its administrator. The administrator invites and removes members, appoints other administrators, closes and deletes lists and, when a member leaves the group or deletes their account, can confirm on that member's behalf the payments received.
  • Group members see your nickname, your photo and what you do in the group: items you added, purchases, prices and payments.
  • Prices and items are typed by the members themselves. The app does not check whether they are right.
